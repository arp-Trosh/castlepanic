Castle Panic: result of the first session (2026-10-02)
=====================================================

Castle Panic is playable end to end: run `.venv/bin/python -m castlepanic` from the project folder. You get a
menu, single player with 1-6 seats (1 is the solo variant), Host on port 5555 with a lobby, or Join. Everything
has only been tested off-screen, never in a real terminal or on Windows, and I couldn't hear any of the sounds.

What's tested
-------------
- Rules: 300 all-bot games finish with every card and token accounted for.
- Networking: host and join, the lobby, bots filling empty seats, chat, turns played over the network, an
  out-of-turn move rejected without a crash, and a player who leaves being replaced by a bot.
- 3D game: two complete bot games played through the 3D scene without anything hanging.
- Screen: a scripted run of the menu and game screens, with screenshots.

What's in it
------------
- Rules: the full game plus the solo variant (hand of 6, discard up to 2). Everything is also written out in a
  log, so you can follow the game without the graphics.
- 3D: a painted mat on a dark wooden table, a forest ring of trees, and towers flying banners in their arc's
  colour. Sentries on the walls fidget while idle.
- Animations: monsters march in, archers loose arrows, and knights and the barbarian charge out. Hits and deaths
  draw blood, walls and towers crumble into rubble with dust, and the Giant Boulder rolls across crushing
  everything in its path. The camera swings to each action.
- Assets: 21 original models in the low-poly, grim style, built by Opus subagents from Blender scripts using a
  shared style guide (assets/src/kit.py, assets/STYLE.md), exported to castlepanic/data/models/.
- Sound: 32 synthesized sounds (horns, bow twangs, growls, yells, crashes, the boulder's rumble) with nothing
  extra to install. `python -m castlepanic.sound` plays them all.
- Controls: number keys or clicks for cards, letters or clicks for targets, X discard, T trade, E end turn,
  H help, chat on Tab in multiplayer.

Problems and gaps
-----------------
- Frame rate: about 50-90 ms per frame mid-game, roughly 11-20 fps. Most of it is in the engine (see below).
- Bots: they win only about 16% of games: 36% solo, about 2% with 6 players. That's after a lookahead
  improvement, and they can get better.
- Unconfirmed rule details: the rule pages don't give the mix of special monster tokens or the bosses' hit
  points. Both are my best reconstruction (move_<colour> x2 each, draw3 x1, draw4 x1; King 2, Warlord 3,
  Mage 3, Healer 2 HP); the deck matches published counts.
- Models: some read dark at whole-board zoom. A few hands don't grip their weapon handles, and the orc warlord
  is murky.
- Asset agents: they ran on Opus but not at low effort, because the agent definition that sets low effort
  (.claude/agents/asset-builder.md) only loads in a new session.
- Not done: Windows packaging.

Engine findings so far (all in ENGINE_NOTES.md)
-----------------------------------------------
- The biggest one: the renderer works out every model part's position in plain Python, costing about 55 µs per
  part per frame. With characters at 50-90 parts each, that takes most of the frame. (Now at the top of
  ../unicode3droadmap.md as the next patch.)
- There's no way to copy an animated model cheaply, so I wrote that in the game.
- There's no blending between animation clips, so I wrote that too.
- Model.world_bounds() costs about 5 ms per call.
- merge_meshes drops textures.
- Screen text is limited to the 8 basic colours.
- The terminal-screenshot helper isn't part of the public API.
- Texture v orientation for hand-built meshes (Mesh.uvs) is undocumented.
